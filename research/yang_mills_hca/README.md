# Yang–Mills-inspired equivariant HCA / AWR

**Independent Elpis research. Frozen shadow evidence; NOT production-admitted.**

This is the *current* Elpis geometric HCA research line. It is **not** the older ECS_C typed-hypergraph HCA comparator at [`native/ECS_C/hca`](../../native/ECS_C/hca) / [`docs/ECS_C_HCA.md`](../../docs/ECS_C_HCA.md). The older comparator remains in the repository as distinct historical research; we do not rewrite its implementation or qualification.

The local Yang–Mills/AWR studies R64–R117 use an **88-trainable-parameter equivariant recurrent HCA**, reconstructed as six frozen R77/R78 checkpoints. Reported R114–R116 results support a *zero-new-trainable-parameter* checkpoint-conditioned shadow controller. The large sparse AWR and native production integration remain proposed work, not qualified software.

Read [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md), [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md), and [EVIDENCE_INDEX.json](EVIDENCE_INDEX.json).

**R118 update (research engineering, not promotion):** [`r118_scalable_awr/`](r118_scalable_awr/README.md) authenticates the frozen sources, reconstructs the six checkpoints from a clean checkout (R77 evaluation replays bitwise; the R78 result archive replays byte-identically), and implements an executable bounded sparse AWR substrate (1–50,000 addressable instances, 1,000,000-relation stress registry) that invokes the authentic frozen HCA. The hot path is latency-qualified for its registered workload with a disclosed regime exception. R93 remains `ENERGY_NORMALIZED_DENOMINATOR_HOLD`; local transition fidelity is not solved.

**R119 update:** [`r119_numerical_hotpath/`](r119_numerical_hotpath/README.md) traces R118-D's remaining latency exception (exact arithmetic on decayed states entering the subnormal range) to specific operations and closes it for the default exact path with a guarded kernel that is bit-identical to the original. Opt-in flush-to-zero is not numerically qualified. R93 is unchanged.

**R122 update (diagnostic, not promotion):** [`r122_energy_direction_forensics/`](r122_energy_direction_forensics/README.md) localizes the long-horizon normalized error on six new topology classes under a protocol registered before measurement: predominantly accumulated drift from a decayed-regime operator that dissipates too little, with denominator collapse confined to readout zero crossings and controller-induced sign reversals for R114 and SIGNED_ONLY. R120 (`r120_checkpoint_policy/`) and R121 (`r121_temporal_topology/`) remain bounded research passes. R93 is unchanged.

**Incremental update (ELPIS_YM_AWR_R120, engineering, not promotion):** [`r120_incremental_geometric_update/`](r120_incremental_geometric_update/README.md) adds in-place, transactional, gauge-covariant updates of existing AWR relation states with frozen CGC coefficients, fused with selective exact propagation through the R119 kernel. It is unrelated to the R120 checkpoint-selection study despite the shared number. Mechanically qualified and bit-exact; latency targets met only with the exact parallel pass; CGC weights are synthetic and their utility is not established. R93 is unchanged.
