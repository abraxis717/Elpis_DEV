## Scientific problem

**HCA-specific proposition to eventually test:** At matched information, model bytes, training examples, CPU/memory and total work, a learned typed, local, recurrent HCA can provide an independently measured advantage in *dynamic* structural candidate inference or damage/local-update recovery over (i) an exact incremental typed closure/index (B0I), (ii) a learned typed factor-node message-passing network with equally expressive n-ary edges and cached state, and (iii) simpler trainable symbolic/graph rules. The claim must be falsifiable; no guarantee of superiority is assumed.

Separate three problems, never mix their claims:

- **H1A compositional anti-shortcut assay:** fixed typed hypergraph with fully visible admissible relations; binary query on an unseen, randomly renamed graph. Model input is only `graph` and `query`. This measures structural sensitivity, not a useful advantage over exact logic.
- **H1B dynamic source-and-binding assay (next DEV stage):** bounded graph streams with source validity changes, explicit admission/revocation, held bindings, varying candidate/ADMITTED statuses, conflicting evidence, locality/interference and damage. Measure change-sensitive end-to-end prediction, stale-source errors, retained activity and real incremental work. Compare to *incremental B0I* and warm-started learned factor-node inference, not unfair fresh global recomputation.
- **H1C genuinely incomplete-evidence candidate prediction (separate future design):** learned probabilistic or compositional structural information not fully determined by supplied exact rules. Must beat train-only motif/rule induction, exact keyed storage, and equal-information typed factor graph. Labels and missingness must be generated without leakage from source labels or identity artifacts. No native semantic-authority promotion.

### H1A frozen DEV generator (implemented)

All content is synthetic, pure-stdlib and deterministic (SHA-256 counter-derived seeds). One graph has 20 cells, 20 factors, one scope, source states VALID or STALE, two typed ternary chain relations and 16 unrelated distractor factors. No factor or graph exceeds native HCA ABI v1 size limits. For each opposing pair, the *entire* node set, identity list, source states, relation/status histogram, per-node kind/source/degree profile, seed set and query target are the same. Only the assignment of the output role in two R2 factors changes; the exact gold label must flip. Pair output positions are not passed to a learner.

An independent exact closure starts with the public seed nodes, admits only VALID/ADMITTED edges of relation types 1 and 2 and iterates until fixpoint. The scorer, not any model-facing data, owns the labels for DEV. The ablation controls make one intermediate source STALE; both pair sides then have gold 0. They check refusal of invalid support, not positive/negative structural discrimination.

Split sizes:

| Partition | Structural opposing-label pairs | Source-ablation pairs | Rows |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| TRAIN | 128 | 16 | 288 labeled |
| DEV | 64 | 16 | 160 public and 160 separately stored labels |

All node IDs are SHA-256 digests scoped to `(split, family, pair, local role)` and disjoint between TRAIN and DEV. Rows are independently shuffled, including DEV gold, so a model cannot rely on adjacency or pair-side ordering. Train and DEV are developmental; **no QUAL rows are generated**.

**H1A mandatory controls (implemented):**

1. Known-rule exact closure: privileged upper bound (may not be given to learned models as side-channel instructions).
2. Train-only symbolic rule induction: fit a fixed 10-member family of single- and two-relation-type closure sets using TRAIN labels only, then evaluate on DEV. This can learn the generator's exact rule and is **not an HCA model**.
3. Constant no-graph majority predictor: fitted only on TRAIN labels.
4. Relation-marginal/degree-only null: within structural pairs, identical input features force the same answer for opposite labels, hence cannot get both sides right.
5. Independent audit of graph label recomputation, paired intervention, split isolation, no DEV public labels, exact counts, canonical ordering and all metadata.

**Interpretation gate:** If the simple train-only symbolic rule inducer achieves 100% on DEV, the assay is only a necessary unit test of structural compositional generalization; it is **not** a discriminating scientific task. Do **not** train B3 and claim HCA advantage from success on this assay. Proceed to H1B design instead. No threshold may be revised to conceal this. A symbolic baseline success is a valid negative scientific-design finding, not a mechanical failure.
